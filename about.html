Background and Story

About Me

I grew up watching how small daily decisions keep a business alive. Helping with our family enterprise in Nigeria taught me early on that greeting a customer well, keeping accurate ledgers, and reordering stock on time are all connected. Over three years of marketing and commercial work, I learned to pay attention to what buyers actually ask for and why certain items move faster than others.

Enrolling in the BSc Data Science programme at MIVA Open University gave me a formal language for the patterns I had long observed by hand. Instead of relying on guesswork alone, I now clean transaction sheets, build visual summaries, and write beginner Python scripts to test assumptions before acting on them.

Timeline

From Shop Floor to Data Science

Key stages connecting my commercial work with my university studies.

  1. Early Business Involvement

    Learning Customer Service and Sales Basics

    Started supporting sales counters, recording daily receipts, and helping customers compare products. This stage built my habit of listening carefully to buyer feedback and tracking what sold each week.

  2. Family Business Management

    Managing Stock, Suppliers, and Marketing

    Took active responsibility for inventory planning, promotional outreach, and customer retention over three years of marketing experience. I saw how missed stock counts or poorly timed discounts hurt margins, which pushed me to look for clearer ways to track sales.

  3. MIVA Open University

    Starting BSc Data Science and Analytics

    Began formal studies in Data Science while building practical projects in Excel, Power BI, and Python. I now combine business intuition with clean quantitative evidence to guide stocking, segmentation, and marketing choices.

Direction

Career Aspirations

My goal is to work in marketing analytics, business analysis, and business intelligence. Many growing businesses collect sales receipts and customer messages every day, yet rarely have someone who understands both the commercial context and the spreadsheets behind it.

By combining three years of marketing experience with formal Data Science training, I aim to help organisations make calm, evidence based decisions. Whether analysing product performance, tracking repeat customer intervals, or planning stock budgets, I want every number I present to translate directly into a practical next action.
